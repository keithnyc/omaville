# Infrastructure art prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used (no CLI/API fallback). Final assets are in the four folders listed below; images have genuine alpha and are normalized to bottom-centered 256x256 canvases.

## Shared direction

Production Omaville city-builder sprites. Match existing commercial/industrial/residential references: elevated orthographic camera, lower-left-facing front, warm crisp detailed 32-bit pixel art, readable near 32 px. Isolated subject with actual transparent alpha and tiny contact shadow. No text, vehicles, people, roads, checkerboard, or broad background glow. Buildings have no ground slab; parks retain only paths/sand underneath their features.
